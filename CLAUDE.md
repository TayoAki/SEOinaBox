# SEO in a Box

A white-labelled product that gives a Client's website a team of AI Bots that do SEO work, built on CopilotKit's OpenBot. Planning runs on the wayfinder map, the GitHub issue labelled `wayfinder:map`. Research notes are in `docs/research/`.
